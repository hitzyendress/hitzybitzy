Before you book

Good to know

Do I need a consultation first?

Not for every service, but a $27 consultation is a great choice if you're new to a treatment or want to talk through options first.

Is a deposit required?

Some services take a deposit when you book. Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable, so please pick a time you can keep.

What's a Good Faith Exam?

A short health review with a licensed provider that some injectable treatments may require. You'll be told when booking if yours needs one.

How long do results last?

It depends on the treatment, your skin and your lifestyle. I'll go over what to expect for your treatment at your visit.
